The Legend of Mainland

The Mission

Eighteen days of war ended an age. Hastinapur still stands, but hollow — its throne empty, its halls quiet in the way a place goes quiet after too much grief. Something old stirred in the silence beneath the palace ruins, and when it woke, it took the Brahmastra down into the dark with it. There is no army left to send after it. There is only Arjun — descending alone through six corrupted floors toward one last battle to take it back.

Allies You'll Meet — One Per Floor

Draupadi
Sage — Floor 1

Wise and compassionate, she carries ancient lore others have forgotten — and sometimes, a key.

Shakuni
Trickster — Floor 2

His advice may help. It may mislead. He enjoys the uncertainty either way.

Bhima
Berserker Ally — Floor 3

Blunt, enthusiastic, and usually a few kills ahead of you already.

Karna
Rival Warrior — Floor 4

Proud, and no friend of Arjun's — but he respects strength enough to gift the Vasavi Shakti, an arrow that answers only once.

Gandhari
Blind Oracle — Floor 5

She sees without eyes, and speaks in riddles about a fate she already knows.

Floor 6 introduces no one. The calm before the storm.

The Final War — Floor 7

There is no floor beneath the last one. Only the Field of Kurukshetra itself, remembered into being — first the Kaurava line rushing in ranks, then their elite guard, then Duryodhana making his last stand, and finally Kali — the demon of this dark age, older than memory, darker than death.

Ordinary enemies come in kinds now — some chase, some keep their distance and strike from range, some dash in bursts, and some scorch the ground you stand on. A rare few are elite: they'll shrug off steel once badly wounded, and only the Vasavi Shakti can finish what your blade can't.

The Realms

The Outer Ruins (Floors 1–2) — once the outer wall of Indraprastha, now home to things that remember only hunger.
The Blood Crypts (Floors 3–4) — where Duryodhana's court was buried rather than kneel.
The Void Sanctum (Floors 5–6) — past the crypts, where the world stops obeying its own rules.
The Field of Kurukshetra (Floor 7) — the war itself, one last time.
